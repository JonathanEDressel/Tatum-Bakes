Our Story

A little kitchen with a whole lot of heart

TatumBakes started the way the best things do — with a well-loved oven, a hand-me-down whisk, and an excuse to bake for the people we love. What began as holiday cookies for friends has grown into a cozy home bakeshop serving custom treats for birthdays, showers, weddings, and every season in between.

Everything is baked to order in small batches using real butter, quality chocolate, and a generous pinch of patience. No shortcuts — just honest, homemade goodness made fresh for your celebration.
